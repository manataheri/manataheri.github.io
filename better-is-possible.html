Better Is Possible Design Challenge

How might we build problem-solving capabilities across Steelcase's global network and use them to tackle pressing social and environmental issues?

I conceptualized, designed, and led the Better Is Possible Design Challenge, a global innovation workshop that brought together employees from offices and plants, clients, and nonprofit partners to work on a pressing challenge, and along the way built human-centered problem-solving capabilities across the network.

I owned the program end to end: concept, design, pilot, evaluation, organizational rollout, and budget. I also managed a cross-functional team of communications and technical staff, facilitators around the world, and external vendors.

I launched the pilot four months after joining Steelcase. As a newcomer, I had to win the support of leaders who barely knew me, and 11 of them agreed to host the pilot at their offices, in cities including Shanghai, Sydney, Grand Rapids, Monterrey, and Cluj. Within a year, the program ran in 15 cities, with 72 organizations and more than 300 participants.

Designing the materials was an exciting challenge, because I was designing for two groups at once. The facilitators needed to feel confident leading the workshop at their office, even though many had never led one before or had never worked with design thinking. The participants came from different backgrounds and needed materials that were easy to follow. And every workshop had to meet the same standard while leaving room for local adaptation.

Event guide

For facilitators

Facilitator’s kit

For facilitators

Step-by-step instructions and tips for running the workshop: how to prepare the space, what happens in each phase, and how to help teams get unstuck.

During the workshop

3

Instructional videos

For participants and facilitators

Short videos played for participants at each location, introducing each phase of the problem-solving process and its method, so facilitators of any experience level can run the session.

4

Recap slides

For participants

Slides summarizing each video’s key points and the micro-timing for every activity, shown during teamwork so teams can refer back as they go.

5

Team worksheets

For participants

Templates and examples that guide teams through each phase and help them capture their key findings.

After the workshop

6

Facilitator feedback calls

For facilitators

Calls with facilitators to capture their experience and gather points for improvement.

7

Participant follow-up calls

For participants

An overview of the solutions that came out of offices all over the world, and encouragement to implement them.
