Hi, I'm Mana!

I help organizations
make sense of complexity
and move through change.

I bridge strategy, design, facilitation, and execution to design learning experiences that change how people work. I draw from toolkits such as norm design, design thinking, learning design, and speculative design.

I was born in Tehran and am based in Berlin. I've worked from Havana to Istanbul to New York, and cultural humility runs through everything I do. You can find some of my work here.

Work with me

From global corporates to academic institutions and social-impact programs, I’ve partnered with a wide range of organizations and teams. Here is how we could work together.

  • Norm design

    I help organizations see what gets in the way of the change they want, whether that’s faster AI adoption or more collaboration. I surface the unwritten rules at play, co-create interventions with your people, and measure the impact.

  • Leadership development

    I design hands-on learning formats that give leaders practical tools to work differently, apply them in their own business context, and track their progress.

  • Facilitation

    I help teams navigate complexity and align on a shared vision, whether in strategy workshops with leaders, innovation projects, team retreats, or co-creation sessions with stakeholders.

  • Culturally responsive learning design

    Drawing on my PhD research and years of international work as a learning designer, I help you design programs that fit your context, from curriculum and teaching strategies to the learning space itself.

  • Train the trainer

    I prepare your internal facilitators to deliver your programs consistently and adapt them to their own context.

  • Strategic foresight

    I help organizations spot early signals of change, imagine where they might lead, and prepare strategies before the future arrives.

  • Graphic recording and illustration

    I listen closely and capture the key ideas from meetings and conferences in simple visuals that make complex concepts easy to grasp and share.
